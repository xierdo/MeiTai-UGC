# Video production rules

## Reference roles
Map images in the actual order supplied, never assume a permanent @Image1–4 convention across SKUs. Common roles: (a) original body and label (b) pump / cap mechanics (c) hand scale and actual liquid/cream (d) hair condition or other visual reference. Write each reference independently so every prompt is standalone. A product-only image does not define a person or background. Use the specified half of a comparison image only for that hair condition; never reproduce a mannequin's face or bare body. Where possible crop the two hair panels separately and use the same approved realistic human reference. Do not claim cropping or reference binding has been performed unless it has.

## Product invariants
Use actual SKU, volume, colors, packaging, hand-to-product scale, spout, label layout, liquid appearance and application sequence. Do not make up label text not visible or supplied. Do not mix products. For a closed-pack demonstration, omit irrelevant dispensing and chemistry. If pumping, support the bottle on a surface or use a plausible grip; show correct downward motion, stream from side outlet and palm below. White foam develops with water, not from a regular shampoo pump. Use only verified product instructions; when unspecified do not invent exposure time or other label directions.

Project-specific starting examples (not a complete or authoritative SKU database; current images and confirmed product facts override):
- FLUFFY CLEAN OIL 450ml: translucent pink pump bottle, clear/colorless gel; show no pink liquid.
- CAVIAR LUXURY CARE Soft Shampoo 450ml: matte-black pump bottle, pearl/milky-white shampoo. The unlocked stem is white in supplied reference. Never confuse with clear oil or hair mask.
- Roe Soft Hair Mask 300ml: short black wide jar with reflective gold outer screw lid; separate white inner seal if present in reference. Apply per verified instructions to damp lengths/ends and rinse; no invented leave-on duration. Confirm cream color and particles from current reference; don't transfer another mask's color.
- SILKY AND SUPPLE Anti-Dandruff Shampoo 450ml: mint-green bottle, dark-green pump and pearly milky-white shampoo in supplied references. Do not turn shampoo green or promise dandruff cure.
- Coconut Oil Hair Care 100ml: for this user's content apply only to completely dry, cooled hair from mid-lengths to ends, not scalp. No unsupported heat-protection claims. Verify packaging and oil texture from current images.

## Visual state locks
For oily/flat hair: BEFORE dry but greasy (not water-wet), narrow oily clumps, low crown tightly following skull, hair hanging near temples; AFTER fully dry, root lift and visibly rounded airy crown, separated strands and smooth lengths. No artificial density, extensions or frizz to simulate volume.
For rough/frizzy hair: BEFORE dry rough irregular outer silhouette, scattered flyaways and dry-looking untidy ends; AFTER fully dry, neatly aligned, silky-looking strands, smoother outline, tidy ends, natural non-greasy sheen. No change to haircut, density or length; do not fake difference through lighting or smiling.
For flakes: use irregular off-white small thin flakes, not round beads, glitter, insects or snow overlay; realistic gravity, gentle motion, no injury. Do not use synthetic differences as real proof of dandruff treatment. Label dramatizations and only use genuine substantiated comparisons for results claims.
For matched comparison: same character, head position, wardrobe, focal length/distance, light, motion, background. Show crown through relevant ends; leave headroom. Same neutral expression if helpful. Hold both completed states long enough to read. If both hairstyles look similar with face hidden, redesign the visual, don't merely add adjectives. Separate approved hair-state clips; assemble split screen from actual clips in editing when needed.

## Timing patterns (adapt to requested duration)
- 9–12s: one hook/product moment + one hair or product-use moment + short Thai CTA. No forced full wash routine or several lines of dialogue.
- 15–20s: one visible problem + product/one or two routine inserts + after reveal if evidence supports the depiction. Use clear time jumps. Prefer final payoff >=3s; comparison optional, not mandatory.
- 20–30s: one situation, a short exchange, practical care logic, product recommendation or natural action. 2–3 speakers; avoid overly dense lines. Start with the interesting action instead of a long establishing shot. Use one continuous shot only when location and action permit.
- In any format, no instant dry-to-wet-to-dry morph. Full wash/rinse/dry needs separate edited moments; allow realistic off-screen time. Do not show unrelated actions or movement requiring an extra hand.

## Camera and sound
Phone-level exposure, slight natural handheld sway, realistic autofocus and skin texture; no excessive shake, gimbal commercial shots or artificial beauty filters. POV: specify who holds phone and product; if both hands are required, use another camera operator or stable placement. Third-person: identify friend filming, camera path, character gaze and spatial layout. Use ordinary Thai homes, salons, shops or public spaces without implying real partnerships. For conversations, natural ambient sounds and low/no music. Show price/Thai signage only if legibility needed and facts provided; avoid invented specific prices and unreadable prominent text. Optional Thai overlays added in editing. Keep dialogue exact, no invented extra speech.

## Model adaptation
When the requested runtime, references, lip-sync or single-shot mode are unsupported or uncertain, write modular segments and explain the limitation; do not promise a particular Kling, Jimeng or Topview feature without verification. For production constraints use the user-provided cap; when unknown, default compact and offer a longer version only if useful. Never claim a video has been generated unless it actually has.
