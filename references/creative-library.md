# Reusable creative modules

Select a few; these are hypotheses, not proven winners. For a batch, hold the product, factual benefit and audience constant if testing hook/format.

| Situation / segment | Visual hook | Natural story or payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Frequently colored hair | Hold visibly rough ends | Ask stylist how to care for lengths; generic product recommendation |
| Long-haired commuter | Hair trapped under bag strap | Friend helps free hair gently; practical care question |
| Friends at café | Ask permission to touch hair | Talk about routine, save product info |
| Beauty shopper | Confused at shelf, checks rough ends | Staff explains two-step routine; items enter basket |
| Siblings at home | Borrow a bottle | Playful conversation about correct product use |
| Oily flat roots | Lift roots with fingers and release | Matched crown silhouette illustration; no added density |
| Dandruff concern | Visible flakes on dark clothing | Product routine and non-guaranteed care explanation |
| Frizzy lengths | Gentle comb encounters resistance | Matched comb or strand movement demonstration with real evidence |
| Outdoor casual UGC | One-handed bottle display | Selfie shows styled hair, short natural CTA |
| Post-gym or helmet commuter | Disordered hair after removing gear | Daily routine, convenience, no impossible rapid transformation |

## Functional story skeletons
- Problem -> practical care advice -> product choice -> action.
- Visual question -> reaction -> product fact -> next step.
- Product display -> hair appearance / application -> CTA.
- Matched visual test -> authentic routine -> genuine evidence or clearly labeled illustration.
- Conversation -> credible recommendation -> decision (e.g., ask link or place products in basket).

## Atomic assets
Save product shots (front, hand, pump, texture), character/wardrobe IDs, setting, hook footage, routine actions, payoff, voice/CTA, and edit notes separately if the workflow supports it. Track SKU and usage state; reuse only compatible footage. A generated clip of a different person cannot become the same-person after clip. Keep original asset provenance. Do not assume every scene needs every product reference; use only the relevant ones while preserving supplied mapping.
